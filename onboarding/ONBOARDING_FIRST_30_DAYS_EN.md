## The principle

This path is not invented. Patrick described it on the leadership call of 16.08 as
something already running for two weeks: 14 days → five direct active partners →
closed group → closing calls. This document does not create the path. It writes it
down, so Patrick no longer has to explain it to every new person by voice.

The problem it solves is the one Patrick himself named twice: a newcomer does not know
when the earning starts. Until now the answer was "eventually". Now it is a specific
threshold and a specific amount.

---

## STEP 1 · The first 14 days

**Your task: bring five direct active partners.**

Not ten. Five.

**What to do**

- Write down the people you already talk to. Not a "target audience" — just the people
  you can call.
- For each of them: a short conversation, then the link.
- Do not explain everything yourself. Your job is to spark interest and set a time.

**What is not expected of you**

Knowing how to sell. Knowing every number. Answering hard questions.
You will learn that along the way — and not on your own.

---

## STEP 2 · You are in

Five direct active partners done — you are in the team's closed group.

**Weekly leader call.** What is working right now, real situations, your questions
answered.

**Closing calls.** You bring the person and set the time — Patrick joins the call.
He leads the conversation.

> This is the main thing you get on entry.
> You do not need to know how to close. You need to bring people and set the time.

---

## STEP 3 · The first money

| | |
|---|---|
| **Condition** | $5,000 first-line volume, under the 50/50 rule |
| **Window** | 14 days from joining the group `⟨PL?⟩` |
| **Reward** | $200 in cash, personally from Patrick |

**What the 50/50 rule means.** One person counts for half at most. Of the $5,000, no more
than $2,500 may come from your strongest leg — the rest has to come from others. One large
deposit does not clear the threshold.

**Where the money comes from.** This is not a company payout and not a program bonus.
It is Patrick's own decision — so that you have money for coffee and fuel while you are
out there talking to people.

---

## STEP 4 · Beyond

This is where the point of it all begins: your structure grows in depth without your
daily involvement.

Conditions, percentages and levels live in your back office. They change, and your back
office is the only reliable place to read them. Not someone else's screenshot, not
someone's retelling.

---

## The rules of the circle

Those who move, move with us. Those who don't, make room.

Nobody runs five projects at once. Volume spread across different programs builds nothing.

`⟨PL?⟩` — taken almost word for word from the leadership call. Check the tone:
this may be too hard for a first touch.

## Deliberately left out

**Promo accounts.** Discussed on the 16.08 call as a tool for newcomers ($500 / $1,000
package against 15× volume). Yakov's decision: not in the handout. It is an internal
leadership tool, granted by hand and by decision. On the same call Patrick described the
risk: people who miss the volume target hit "withdraw", get refused, screenshot it and
spread that the broker is a scam.

**Percentages and rates.** On the 16.08 call four top leaders spent twenty minutes unable
to agree whether their profit share is 2.5% or 1.5%, each looking at his own back office.
While that is the case, no percentages go into material for newcomers.

**Risk disclaimer.** Per Yakov's decision, not at this stage. Revisit before publication

## Appendix · grounding in the sources

**The funnel** — leadership call 16.08, Patrick Lehner (18:53): a person enters through
the promo; the condition is to bring not ten directs but five active ones within 14 days;
after that he enters the closed WhatsApp group, where there is a weekly leader call, and
where members can schedule closing Zoom calls that Patrick joins. Running for about two
weeks, with results.

**The $200** — same call (21:43): Patrick's own promotion for those already in the group.
$2,500 first-line under the 50% rule — that is $5,000 of volume — and he pays $200 in cash,
"damit er zumindest für die nächsten zwei Wochen seinen Kaffee bezahlt hat, wenn er
herumläuft und mit den Leuten redet".

**The entry problem** — Zoom 03.08 (16:24): Yakov — "Wir müssen einen Weg für die Neuen
zeigen, wo die am Start verdienen. Das ist der einzige Schwachpunkt."
Patrick — "Das ist der wesentliche Punkt, die Leute ins Feuer zu bringen."
Same call (16:22), he admitted he had not done the maths: "Das muss ich mal durchrechnen."

**Depth duplication** — leadership call (20:16): the first line is not the problem,
everyone knows how to build it. Real duplication in his English team only began between
lines three and five; the team is now on line thirteen and line ten is duplicating.
Hence Step 4: the path does not end at five directs.

**The filter** — same call (33:09): people running five projects at once and spreading
their volume get removed from the structure. "Entweder ganz oder gar nicht."

**The Academy** — Zoom 03.08 (13:10, 44:21): modules — mindset, sales and onward, repeated
in cycles so newcomers can catch up. The reference structure Patrick sent: eight steps,
"my start · what to do · your partner · who helps me". Not expanded here — that is the
next stage.
